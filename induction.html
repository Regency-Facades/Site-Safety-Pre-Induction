1. Awareness & GDPR

Welcome to the Regency Facades Site Safety Pre-Induction. It is vitally important that this pre-induction is undertaken and fully understood before you commence any work.

Site management requires certain personal and medical details to ensure we can keep you safe and contact your next of kin in an emergency. Proper data collection standards mean your information is explicitly consented to, protected, and used strictly for site safety and emergency protocols.

2. Medication Disclosure

You must inform site management of any medication you are taking, regardless of whether you believe it has a "no working effect".

Sharing details of prescribed or over-the-counter medication (especially those that cause drowsiness or impair reaction times) is mandatory. In the event of an emergency, knowing exactly what medication you are on before intervention could save your life when emergency workers respond.

3. Medical Conditions & Sensitivities

You must inform management of any medical conditions or sensory sensitivities (e.g., loud noises, high-decibel drills) upon pre-induction.

Disclosing this ensures that site management can provide the proper specialised protection. This information will be incredibly useful and vital if it needs to be provided to an ambulance or responding emergency unit.

4. Drugs & Alcohol Policy

We operate a strict zero-tolerance policy. This covers not just consumption on site, but the severe after-effects of heavy drinking or hangovers.

  • Suspected: If you are only suspected of being under the effects, you will be immediately removed from site and required to undergo a repeat Pre-Induction the next day.
  • Proven: If it is proven you are under the effects of alcohol and/or drugs, you will be immediately removed and never accepted back on site.

5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

The standard 5-point PPE is non-negotiable at all times. Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE) and any other special protection equipment must be worn exactly as per the RAMS requirements.

Daily Inspection: PPE must be inspected every single morning and will require regular renewal.

Never borrow damaged PPE: If your equipment is broken or defective, report it to your supervisor immediately for replacement. Do not take your mate's PPE.

6. Working at Height

There are strict rules against unauthorized tampering with scaffolding, access platforms, or structure joints.

Trades working at height: You are to make NO alterations to safe working platforms. If they are not suitable, contact site management and request the needed changes.

Scaffolders & IPAF Trained: You are NOT to adjust, build, or dismantle any safe working platforms (scaffolding, mobile towers, delta decks, podiums, etc.) without direct directives from site management to do so!

7. Fire Emergency Response

Upon arrival on site, check and familiarise yourself with the fire rescue plan, including escape routes, extinguisher locations, alarms, and the muster point.

Fire extinguishers are NOT for putting down the fire! They are there to help you safely leave an area that is not safe. Only if you are trained, and it is safe to do so because it is a very small fire, should you try to put it down.
Do not go back inside unless you have been told by site management explicitly that it is safe to do so. Operatives who fail to evacuate will be removed from site immediately and will need a repeat Pre-Induction next day.

8. Emergency Response

In a life-threatening emergency, call 999 first thing! The seconds spent finding the first aider may save the injured life!

  • Non-life-threatening: Find a way to raise the alarm (ask a colleague to do so) and find the First Aider. They can be recognised by a green cross on their hard hat.
  • First Aid Kit: The first aid kit, including the eye wash station, is located in the site office.
  • Reporting: All injuries and incidents are and need to be reported! Near misses can be reported anonymously via scanning the QR code from posters that are all over the site.

9. General Site Rules

To keep the site running safely and efficiently, the following rules apply to everyone:

  • Signing In/Out: You must sign in and out every time you go out—even if you went for a coffee at the shop two minutes away!
  • Warning Notices: Observe warning notices and comply with them. They are not decorating the site; they are there to provide clear information about potential hazards.
  • Good Housekeeping: Keeping your work area tidy is a must. This saves us all lives in an emergency situation, and also saves our resources that would be otherwise wasted to clear off the mess in the end.

10. COSHH & Environmental Protection

Everything that is important to know generally about Control of Substances Hazardous to Health (COSHH) and environmental protection related to our construction site.

  • All chemicals and hazardous substances must be used and stored exactly as dictated by their COSHH assessments.
  • All waste must be segregated into the correct designated skips (e.g., Timber, Metal, General Waste).
  • Spills of oil, fuel, or chemicals must be contained immediately using site spill kits and reported to management.
